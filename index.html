Karthikeya Kusuma

I study how molecules behave, and I work in the clinics where that knowledge has to land.

I'm a junior at the University of Washington studying Biochemistry and Public-Global Health. I build machine-learned models for molecular dynamics, run health research on clinical trial and population data, and work in a neurology clinic. I plan to apply to PhD programs in Autumn 2027.
